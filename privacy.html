Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Android game "Sheru Bhai Ka Raaj" ("the game") published by Saikaizen Innovations ("we").

What the game stores on your phone

Your game progress (cash, items, missions, settings) and the photos you take inside the game are saved only on your device. The game has no account, no login and does not upload your progress or photos to us.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). AdMob may fill ads through its mediation partners Meta Audience Network (Meta privacy policy) and Liftoff Monetize, formerly Vungle (Liftoff privacy policy). To show and measure ads, these services may collect and share: device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, app interactions and ad interactions, and diagnostic data. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before requesting ads (Google User Messaging Platform), and you can change your choice any time from Settings › Privacy options in the game. See how Google uses data.

Analytics and crash reports

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google. They collect: how the game is used (for example which screens are opened, missions started and finished, ads loaded and shown), device and operating system information (model, Android version, language, memory size), crash and error traces, an app instance ID generated for this installation, and, where you have consented, the advertising ID. The game also uses Firebase Remote Config to adjust settings such as how often ads may appear, which uses the app instance ID. We use this information only to find and fix bugs, keep the game running well on low-end phones and improve the game. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not sell it.

Where the law requires consent, analytics follows the choice you make in the consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can turn analytics off any time in Settings › Share usage stats, and change your consent in Settings › Privacy options. Crash reports are kept so we can fix crashes. Google keeps this data under its own terms; see Firebase privacy and security and Google's privacy policy.

Google Play services

The game uses Google Play in-app review and in-app update. These are provided by Google Play on your device under Google's privacy policy.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, opt out of ad personalisation there, and delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. We hold no account about you. Analytics and crash data are linked only to a random app instance ID; to ask for that data to be deleted, email us from the address below and include the date you installed the game, or simply uninstall the game, which removes the app instance ID from your phone.

Changes

If the game adds new services or uses data in a new way, this page will be updated before that version is released.
