Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea for the next update? Write to superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com with your phone model and what happened. We read every message.

Common questions

How do I change the language? Phone › Settings › Language.

The buttons are too small or in the wrong place. Settings › Button layout lets you move and resize every button.

Does it need the internet? No. The game plays offline; ads and updates need a connection.
